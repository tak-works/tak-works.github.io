# Privacy Policy for ReSeat

Last updated: November 8, 2026

ReSeat ("we", "our", or "the application") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding information and data handling.

## 1. Zero Data Collection
ReSeat does **NOT** collect, transmit, store, or share any personal information, usage analytics, or telemetry data. 
The application functions completely offline and contains no tracking code or external network communication capabilities.

## 2. Local Storage Only
All window coordinates, application names, and user preferences are stored exclusively on your local device (in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\ReSeat`). This data never leaves your computer.

## 3. Third-Party Access
Because ReSeat does not collect any data, no third parties have access to your information.

## 4. Contact & Support
If you have any questions, feedback, or need support regarding ReSeat or this Privacy Policy, please open an issue on our GitHub repository:
- **Studio**: Tak-Works
- **Website**: [https://tak-works.github.io](https://tak-works.github.io)
- **Support**: [Open an Issue on GitHub](https://github.com/tak-works/tak-works.github.io/issues)

## プライバシーポリシー（日本語要約）

最終更新日: 2026年11月8日

ReSeatは、ユーザーのプライバシーを最重要視して設計されています。

1. **個人情報の非収集**: ReSeatは、個人情報、利用状況データ、テレメトリ等の外部送信や収集を一切行いません。
2. **完全ローカル保存**: ウィンドウの座標や設定情報は、すべてユーザーの端末内（`%LOCALAPPDATA%\ReSeat`）にのみ保存され、外部に送信されることはありません。
3. **外部通信ゼロ**: アプリケーションは完全オフラインで動作します。
